# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis

A Python-based Data Analytics project that explores Superstore sales data to identify business trends, customer behavior, product performance, and profitability.

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze Superstore sales data and extract meaningful business insights using Python and data visualization techniques.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales trends and seasonality.
- Evaluate sales and profit by product category.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Analyze customer segment performance.
- Understand the impact of discounts on profitability.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## 📂 Dataset

The Superstore dataset contains order details, customer information, product categories, sales, quantity, discounts, and profit.

## 📈 Analysis and Visualizations

- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Category-wise sales and profit analysis
- Region-wise performance analysis
- Customer segment analysis
- Discount vs. profit analysis

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- November 2018 recorded the highest monthly sales.
- Technology achieved the highest sales and profit among the product categories.
- Furniture generated comparatively lower profit despite significant sales.
- The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
- Higher discount levels were associated with negative average profit.
- The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales and profit.
