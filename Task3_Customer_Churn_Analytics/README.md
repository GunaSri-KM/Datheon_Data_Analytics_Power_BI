# 📊 Customer Cohort & Churn Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
Customer retention is an important factor in business growth. This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns and identifying customer segments that are more likely to leave a service.

Using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib, the dataset is explored to discover meaningful business insights and understand the factors associated with customer churn.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall customer churn and retention.
- Identify churn patterns across different tenure groups.
- Compare churn rates based on contract types.
- Understand the impact of payment methods on customer churn.
- Identify high-risk customer segments.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## 📂 Dataset
**IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset**

- Total Records: 7,043 customers
- Total Features: 21
- Target Variable: Churn

## 📈 Key Findings

Overall Churn Rate: 26.54%
Highest Tenure Cohort Churn: 47.44%
Highest Contract Churn: 42.71%
Highest Payment Method Churn: 45.29%
High-Risk Segment Churn: 49.37%

## 🔍 Business Insights
1. Customers in their first 12 months show the highest churn rate.
2. Month-to-month contract customers are more likely to leave.
3. Electronic check users have a comparatively higher churn rate.
4. Fiber optic customers without technical support form a high-risk segment.

## 📊 Visualizations
The project includes charts representing:
- Customer Churn by Tenure Cohort
- Churn Rate by Contract Type
- Churn Rate by Payment Method

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Improve onboarding and engagement for new customers.
- Encourage customers to explore long-term contract options.
- Review payment experience and address customer concerns.
- Provide proactive technical support to high-risk customers.
